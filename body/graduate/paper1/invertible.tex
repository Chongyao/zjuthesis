\textbf{The proof of positive definiteness:}
\label{sec: invert}
Without loss of generality, we suppose that the system matrix $\mathbf{H}$ is
symmetric positive definitive. Otherwise, we can add $\alpha \I$ to ensure
its positive definiteness. Obviously, its diagonal blocks
$\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{p}}$ and $\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{q}}$ are also positive definite.

\textcolor{black}{Our preconditioner has the following block strucutre based on Eq.~\ref{eq:preconditioner_structure}:
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{P} & =\begin{bmatrix}
                    \sum_{i=0}^{n}\mathbf{h}_{i} & \mathbf{0}                                                                                                                                                \\
                    \mathbf{0}                   & \overbrace{\operatorname{blk-diag}(\J_{\p,\omega}^\top \mathbf{H}_\p \J_{\p,\omega})+ \J_{\q,\omega}^\top \mathbf{H}_\q \J_{\q,\omega}}^{\mathbf{P}_{22}}
                  \end{bmatrix}.
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
}
where $\mathbf{h}_{i}$ is a diagonal matrix whose diagonal elements
are all greater than $\mathbf{0}$.

Obviously, the first diagonal block is positive definite, thus
$\mathbf{P}$ is positive definite iff $\mathbf{P}_{22}$ is positive
definite.

According to ~\Cref{eq:A}, we point out that
$\J_{\mathbf{p, s}}^{\top}\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{p}}\J_{\mathbf{p, s}}$ and
$\J_{\mathbf{q, s}}^{\top}\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{q}}\J_{\mathbf{q, s}}$ are
both at least positive semi-definite, since:
\begin{equation}
  \forall \mathbf{v} \ne \mathbf{0}, \quad
  \mathbf{v}^{\top}\J_{*}^{\top}\mathbf{H}_{*}\underbrace{\J_{*} \mathbf{v}}_{\mathbf{y}} = \mathbf{y}^{\top}
  \mathbf{H}_{*}\mathbf{y} >= 0.
\end{equation}
Therefore,
$\operatorname{blk-diag}\left(\J_{\p, \omega}^\top \mathbf{H}_{\p} \J_{\p,
    \omega}\right)$ is still semi-definite.
Next, we show that
$\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}^{\top}\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{q}}\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}$ is
positive definite.

The mapping $\mathcal{T}_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}$ from $\omega$ to
$\mathbf{q}$ is a locally injective mapping, so its Jacobian
$\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}$ has full column rank.  Considering the positive
definiteness of $\mathbf{H_{\mathbf q}}$,
%sub-block$\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{q}}$ is also naturally positive definite, and
we can factorize it as $\mathbf{LL}^{\top}$ and
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{rank}(\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}^{\top}\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{q}}\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}})
     & =
    \mathrm{rank}(\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}^{\top}\mathbf{LL}^{\top}\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}) \\
     & =\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf{L}^{\top}\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}})                           \\
     & =\mathrm{rank}(\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}})
     & =\mathrm{dim}(\omega).
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Thus,
$\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}^{\top}\mathbf{H}_{\mathbf{q}}\J_{\mathbf{q,\omega}}$ is
still positive definite.

So far, our preconditioner is positive definite:
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{P} & =\left(\operatorname{blk-diag}\left(\J_{\p, \s}^\top
    \mathbf{H}_{\p} \J_{\p, \s}\right) + \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{0} &

                \mathbf{0}    \\ \mathbf{0} & \J_{\q,\omega}^\top \mathbf{H}_\q \J_{\q,
                  \omega}\end{bmatrix}\right)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  \\
               & =\begin{bmatrix}
                    \overbrace{\sum_{i=0}^{n}\mathbf{h}_{i}}^{\text{positive definite}} & \mathbf{0}                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               \\
                    \mathbf{0}                                                          & \underbrace{\underbrace{\operatorname{blk-diag}(\J_{\p,\omega}^\top \mathbf{H}_\p \J_{\p,\omega})}_{\text{positive semi-definite}}+ \underbrace{\J_{\q,\omega}^\top \mathbf{H}_\q \J_{\q,\omega}}_{\text{positive definite}}}_{\text{positive definite}}
                  \end{bmatrix}.
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
